Made for others

Designs Mira made for someone else. Not part of her own looks, and shared with the blessing of the people they were made for.

For Ma

Ma's Family Table

Made for Mira's mother-in-law: one page holding the family's food preferences, allergies and dates, for her to check before cooking. Designed to look like something her granddaughter made for her. Each face shows the person at the age Ma first knew them: her children stay children, and Mira and her sister-in-law appear as adults.

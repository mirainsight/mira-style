# Ma's Family Table

**Made for** Mira's mother-in-law: one page holding the family's food preferences, allergies, health notes and important dates, for her to check before cooking for everyone.

This is not one of Mira's own looks. It was designed for someone else, and is shared here with her blessing.

## The idea

It is meant to look like something her granddaughter made for her: hand-lettered headings, crayon-bright flowers, warm notebook paper.

Each person appears as a flower. In the real page, a photo sits in the flower's centre, and each face is chosen from the age Ma first knew that person. Her own children stay forever at childhood age; Mira and her sister-in-law appear as adults, because that is when Ma met them.

## Look

- **Paper and ink:** warm notebook paper (`#F7EFDD`), cream cards (`#FFFAEE`), navy ink (`#2B3A67`).
- **Crayon box:** pink `#F2A0B5`, rose `#D9566B`, lilac `#A98BC9`, sun `#F6C453`, orange `#EE8E2B`, sky `#8FB8E8`, leaf `#4F8F6B`, mint `#BFE3CF`.
- **Type:** Patrick Hand for headings (a child's careful handwriting), Nunito for reading.
- **Faces:** daisy, tulip and blossom shapes drawn in SVG, one per person, coloured from the crayon box; a photo replaces the centre when there is one.
- **Alerts:** allergies in a soft red band (`#FBDFDA` with `#8E2430`) so they cannot be missed.

## Example

[`example/`](example) runs on a made-up family (see `example/config.js`) and saves nothing. The real page keeps its notes encrypted in a Google Sheet behind a family passcode.

Word guide & how we counted
  • Member: anyone on our active list. That is everyone marked New, Regular, Irregular or Follow Up.
  • Unique people: everyone who checked in at least once this year. Of the 742, 531 are members today, 161 have since been archived (stopped coming or graduated), and 50 checked in under a name that is not in the database.
  • Regular: comes 3 or more Saturdays a month. Irregular: 2 or fewer. Follow Up: not seen in 2 months.
  • Attendance rate: out of everyone in the group, the share who actually turn up on a normal Saturday.
  • Momentum: is the group getting bigger or smaller? Last 8 Saturdays compared with the 8 before.
  • Service week / CG week: taken from the NarrowStreet calendar. Six Saturdays with no label are counted as Service.
  • Source: the check-in log and the master database, read on 9 Oct 2026. Latest service counted: 3 Oct 2026.
  • Saturday headcount = unique people checked in that day, visitors included.
  • "Drifting" = came to at least half of the 8 services before the last 8, then zero of the last 4.
  • Only totals are shown, except the streak board, which names people who earned it. Names for follow-up go to zone leaders directly.

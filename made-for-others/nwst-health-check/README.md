# NWST Health Check (EOY Attendance Analytics)

**Made for** the NarrowStreet leaders (our youth group): one page that turns a year of Saturday check-ins into a straight answer on how healthy we are, and what to do about it.

This is not one of Mira's own looks. It was designed for someone else, and is shared here with their blessing.

## In Mira's words

**Why this look?**
I wanted it to have a sporty, edgy, editorial report look, because I knew the youth wanted something colourful, vibrant, easy to read, and also actionable. I previously created another Streamlit version (in NWST Health, also in the check-in repo), but no one really used it because there were too many things, and it was hard to access.

**How did it go?**
It went very well. They loved it, and it got them to clean the data. I shared it with senior leadership in end-of-year budget planning and they were impressed. It got the point across.

## Look

- **Gig-poster stack:** a cobalt hero with a torn bottom edge and halftone dots, then a dark ink body. One column that widens to two on desktop. Dark only, by choice.
- **Colour:** ink `#121016`, cream `#F1E7CF`, cobalt `#2450E0`, hot pink `#FF2E88`, with a purple → pink → orange band (`#7B3FD1` → `#FF2E88` → `#FF8A5B`). Yellow `#E0A800` and teal `#1FB598` for status only.
- **Type:** Alfa Slab One for headlines, Rubik Dirt for the stamped numbers and accents, Archivo for reading, DM Mono for labels.
- **Paper bits:** taped cream and pink labels, slightly rotated cards, a pink starburst ("2026 Season Report") and a scrolling pink ticker. A fine noise layer over everything.
- **Straight answers:** every section is a big headline that says the finding, a sentence with the numbers, one chart, and "3 moves" to do next. A clean-up checklist turns the data problems into tick boxes.
- **Motion:** a loader that counts up the check-ins, count-up numbers, cards that rise in, the streak board sliding in. All off with reduced motion.

## Example

[`example/index.html`](example/index.html) is the real page with made-up sample data: invented cells, zones, teams and names, and jittered numbers. It is a single file and saves only its checklist ticks, in your own browser.
